Core commodities

Commodities

Cu

Copper

Copper carries electricity better than any affordable alternative, which makes it the physical basis of the grid, electric transport, data centres and modern defence systems.

  • Essential to semiconductors, electrical wiring and advanced electronics
  • Indispensable to aerospace and defence for conductivity and durability
  • Ore grades have fallen for a generation, so more rock moves for the same metal

Co

Cobalt

Cobalt occurs with copper through the Central African Copperbelt and remains central to high energy density battery chemistry and to several defence and aerospace alloys.

  • Used in semiconductors, chips and high performance alloys
  • A key component of lithium ion batteries used in electric vehicles
  • Concentrated in one region, with refining capacity concentrated in another

Group

Other critical minerals

The Central African Copperbelt and the wider region host a range of minerals that industry and defence supply chains have come to depend on, several of which occur alongside copper and cobalt.

  • Assessed as part of the same exploration and targeting work rather than as separate programmes
  • The company structure is built to hold further critical minerals as they become material
  • Bbabsal is not named for a single metal, and is not defined by one
